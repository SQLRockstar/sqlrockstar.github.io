# ABOUT THOMAS LAROCK

My career and life experiences are best described as follows: I take things that are hard and make them simple for others to understand.

I am a highly experienced data professional with over 25 years of expertise in diverse roles, from individual contributor to team lead. Passionate about simplifying complex challenges for others and leading with empathy, challenging assumptions, and embracing a systems-thinking approach. Strong analytical reasoning skills and expertise to identify trends and opportunities for significant impact. Builder of cohesive teams by breaking down silos resulting in increased efficiencies and collective success. Track record of driving revenue growth, spearheading industry-leading events, and fostering valuable relationships with major tech players like Microsoft and VMware.

You can find examples of my work at my [speaker](http://thomaslarock.com/speaker/), [author](http://thomaslarock.com/author/), and [data expert](http://thomaslarock.com/data-expert/) pages.
