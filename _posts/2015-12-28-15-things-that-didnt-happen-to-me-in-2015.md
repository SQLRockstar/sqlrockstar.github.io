<a href="https://thomaslarock.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/jeep_xmas.jpg" rel="attachment wp-att-17236"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-17236 size-full" src="https://thomaslarock.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/jeep_xmas.jpg" alt="Things that didn't happen to me in 2015" width="600" height="600" /></a>It’s that time of year again. The time when we find many “year-2015-in-review”, “what-to-expect-when-you’re-expecting-2016”, and “resolutions-you-will-not-follow-in-2016” articles littered about the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f99PcP0aFNE" target="_blank">series of tubes known as the internet</a>. It’s only natural for folks to wax nostalgic as 2015 fades into the sunset one final time.

I, however, have <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2010/12/top-ten-things-that-didnt-happen-in-2010/" target="_blank">never</a> <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2011/12/11-things-that-never-happened-to-me-in-2011/" target="_blank">been</a> <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2012/12/things-that-didnt-happen-to-me-in-2012/" target="_blank">one</a> to <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2013/12/13-things-that-didnt-happen-to-me-in-2013/" target="_blank">follow</a> the <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2014/12/14-things-didnt-happen-2014/" target="_blank">crowd</a>.

So as we close out 2015 it’s time for my annual review of things that didn’t happen to me during the previous twelve months.

As always, you’re welcome.
<h6><span style="color: #000000;">Didn’t get caught cheating on my tests</span></h6>
Unlike Volkswagen, <a href="http://www.newsweek.com/2015/12/25/why-volkswagen-cheated-404891.html">who got caught cheating on emissions tests going back over ten years</a>. That’s a LOT of cheating folks. So much cheating that I’m surprised that <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Goodell" target="_blank">Roger Goodell</a> didn’t try to suspend Volkswagen <a href="http://espn.go.com/boston/nfl/story/_/id/13331590/tom-brady-4-game-suspension-upheld-roger-goodell">for 4 games for deflating those results</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t win the World Cup</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
But Team USA did exactly that this past July <a href="http://www.ussoccer.com/stories/2015/07/05/21/19/150705-wnt-v-jpn-game-story">with a 5-2 rout of Japan</a>, showing us that the phrase “play like a girl” now means “<a href="http://www.bustle.com/articles/95044-the-twitter-reactions-to-carli-lloyd-her-amazing-goals-are-evidence-that-we-all-wish">score a hat trick in the first 16 minutes of a World Cup final</a>”.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t watch any Super Bowl commercials</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Because I was in Germany watching the game, and the international broadcast is ad-free. It’s a wonderful way to watch a game, as it gives you time to decompress from the action instead of being wound up for hours. Oh, and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U7rPIg7ZNQ8">having your team win on the final play is a nice thing, too</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t get a flying car</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Despite being promised one for decades, and a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzm6pvHPSGo">commercial reminding us of this failure as humans</a>, cars still require roads. But thanks to the <span style="text-decoration: line-through;">Google</span> <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2015/10/02/google-alphabet-restructuring/">Alphabet</a>, we now have <a href="https://www.google.com/selfdrivingcar/">self-driving cars</a>, which are <a href="http://www.cnn.com/2015/11/13/us/google-self-driving-car-pulled-over/">being pulled over by the police</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t keep my Microsoft SQL Server MVP award</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
That’s because it’s been <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/b/stevengu/archive/2015/10/07/moving-into-the-next-generation-of-the-microsoft-mvp-award.aspx">renamed to the Microsoft Data Platform MVP award</a>. Check out the <a href="https://mvp.microsoft.com/en-us/pages/mvp-award-update">new award categories</a> and pay attention to how many times you see the word “Azure” in the Data Platform award compared to “SQL Server”.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t lose my job to the Cloud</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Despite all the changes to the Microsoft MVP program and its cloud-first focus as a company, I continue to have a job. Of course, I’m <a href="http://www.solarwinds.com/head-geeks/thomas-larock.aspx">not a DBA anymore either</a>. Those items may be related.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t find life on Mars</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
But we did find <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/press-release/nasa-confirms-evidence-that-liquid-water-flows-on-today-s-mars/">evidence of water</a>, which won’t provide any relief for the folks dying of thirst in California. Did you know it <a href="http://www.mercurynews.com/drought/ci_29239425/california-pot-farms-stealing-water-amid-drought">takes 900 gallons of water to grow one outdoor marijuana plant</a>? I’m now wondering how soon for the <a href="http://www.mirror.co.uk/news/technology-science/technology/astronauts-could-grow-cannabis-red-5177921">first batch of Martian Red</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t lose during the database design throwdown events</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Running my perfect record to 9-0, 2015 saw victories for me at <a href="http://edw2015.dataversity.net/sessionPop.cfm?confid=87&amp;proposalid=7281">Enterprise Data World</a> and the <a href="http://www.sqlpass.org/summit/2015/Sessions/Details.aspx?sid=8439">PASS Summit</a>. And while we aren’t officially booked (yet) for any event in 2016, I am certain we will do it again somewhere in 2016 because I believe in giving Karen (<a href="http://www.datamodel.com/index.php/category/blog/" target="_blank">blog</a> | <a href="https://twitter.com/datachick" target="_blank">@datachick</a>) a chance to finally win one of these debates.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t release a Star Wars themed version of DPA</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Despite the <a href="http://mashable.com/2015/12/08/star-wars-merchandise-parody/#WGEVrhii.SqW">incredible amount of merchandising for The Force Awakens</a>, my company did not release a special version of <a href="http://www.solarwinds.com/database-performance-analyzer-sql-server.aspx">Database Performance Analyzer</a> for the occasion. We did, however, use the opportunity to give away <a href="https://thwack.solarwinds.com/community/solarwinds-community/contests-missions/it-is-your-destiny">some themed t-shirts</a> as well as <a href="https://twitter.com/hashtag/sqlstarwars">some #SQLStarWars tweets</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t choke to death on a Scotch egg</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
While at <a href="http://www.sqlbits.com/">SQLBits</a> last year I found myself on a mission: to consume a Scotch egg from a London pub. It took me most of the week but on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/z8N0TQkc6R/">my last night I found myself a delicious Scotch egg</a>. Yes, I find this exciting enough to list here, partly because the <a href="http://www.markettaverns.co.uk/the_carpenters_arms.html">pub was a near replica of The Winchester</a> from <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0365748/">Shaun of the Dead</a>. Don’t judge me
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t get my data breached</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
Well, not that I know of anyway, thanks to <a href="https://haveibeenpwned.com/">HaveIBeenPwned</a> and Troy Hunt (<a href="http://www.troyhunt.com/" target="_blank">blog</a> | <a href="https://twitter.com/troyhunt" target="_blank">@TroyHunt</a>). But with <a href="http://www.shrm.org/hrdisciplines/technology/articles/pages/why-data-breaches-on-the-rise.aspx">data breaches becoming more common</a> than sand in your shorts at the beach, I’m a bit surprised that my data has been kept safe. Must be that I’m selective about who I do business with online, <a href="https://www.facebook.com/156491659260/photos/pb.156491659260.-2207520000.1450223332./10153248886609261/?type=3&amp;theater">unlike other people in my family</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t get re-elected as PASS President</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
I did not seek an additional term. While eligible to run again I knew it was <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2015/05/i-do-not-choose-to-run-2/">not the right thing for PASS to have one person serve four years</a>. I start my term as Immediate Past President January 1<sup>st</sup>, 2016, for a period of two years, and I am looking forward to the challenges that role will bring.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t burn down my house deep frying a turkey</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
This is mostly due to the fact that I’m not allowed to even attempt to cook the bird in this manner. But if I did, I would be certain to follow <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYkRF_FmD40">all the necessary safety precautions</a>. Besides it’s not like we live in Texas, <a href="http://www.nationaljournal.com/congress/2013/11/27/no-1-state-deep-frying-turkey-accidents-is">which sees about 38 such home fires a year at Thanksgiving</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t buy EMC for $67 billion</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
But I did buy some VMWare this year, so that’s kind of the same thing, I think, because EMC owns VMWare as well. Or they did, but now they don’t. But maybe they do. <a href="http://a16z.com/2015/10/26/dell-emc-vmware/" target="_blank">It’s all rather confusing</a>.
<blockquote>
<h6><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Didn’t close down this blog</strong></span></h6>
</blockquote>
In fact, <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2015/09/blogging-update/">it got a facelift</a>. I’ve even <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/is-not-null-newsletter/">started a newsletter</a>, too. I’m <a href="https://thomaslarock.com/2014/10/happiness-lessons-learned-1000-posts/">over 1,000 posts now</a> and still enjoy writing posts like this one when I find the time.

There you have it, a list of things that didn’t happen to me in 2015.

I can’t wait to see all the things I won’t do in 2016!
